Understanding the Electric Vehicle Market

Explore electric vehicle market performance through interactive visualizations, sales trends, manufacturers, battery types and customer ratings.

About the Project

This project analyzes electric vehicle market data using Tableau Public and presents the published visualizations through a Python Flask web application.

The objective is to explore manufacturer performance, market share, annual sales trends, battery types, country distribution and customer ratings.
